## Overview

The **International Student Centre (ISC)** repository serves as a centralized digital archive for materials curated to assist international students in their transition to and journey through the University of Windsor. This curated collection encompasses critical domains including academic integration, health and wellness, campus safety, and regulatory compliance.

### Repository Purpose

This repository represents a curated collection of resources, instructional videos, and orientation materials. The primary motivation for creating and maintaining this archive is simple yet profound: **to ensure that vital information regarding tenant rights, scam prevention, and campus services remains accessible for continuous reference**.

**Why this repository exists:**

- **Resource Accessibility**: To maintain organized access to essential university and community resources
- **Student Integration**: To support the successful academic and social integration of international students
- **Knowledge Preservation**: To archive critical orientation materials and health safety directives
- **Safety Awareness**: To provide a dedicated portal for information on scam prevention and tenant rights

> [!NOTE]
> All materials were compiled and organized to serve as a supportive resource hub for the international student community during the 2023 academic year.

## Usage Guidelines

This repository is shared to support the international student community and university stakeholders.

**For Students**  
Use these resources as a reference guide for navigating university services, understanding safety protocols, and accessing orientation materials.

**For International Advisors**  
These archived materials may serve as reference points for historical orientation content and departmental resource structuring.

**For Community Partners**  
The documentation on tenant rights and scam prevention provides a baseline for community-integrated student support efforts.
